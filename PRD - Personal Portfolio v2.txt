PRD - Personal Portfolio v2
Project Overview

Design a modern developer portfolio for Myron D'Cruz, a Computer Science student and backend developer.

The portfolio should showcase projects, achievements, technical skills, and provide an easy way for recruiters or clients to get in touch.

The website should feel like exploring a creative developer's digital workspace rather than browsing a traditional portfolio.

Design Direction
Theme

My Digital Workspace

The website should resemble a clean, organized workspace with subtle notebook pages, folders, sticky notes, pinned achievement cards, and desk-inspired layouts.

The theme should feel:

professional
modern
playful
memorable

Avoid making it look cartoonish.

The workspace inspiration should enhance usability rather than dominate it.

Minimal decorative elements

Overall Goal

The portfolio should immediately communicate that Myron D'Cruz is a developer who builds polished, real-world products. Every design decision should support that impression.

The experience should feel memorable because of its thoughtful "digital workspace" theme, not because of excessive decoration or animations. Focus on clarity, craftsmanship, and usability.
